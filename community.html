What this place is

The room the thread leads to.

Most people who feel the ache have nobody to talk to about it. Their friends think they're going through a phase. Their family worries about their soul. The internet is full of people trying to sell them something or convince them they're wrong.

The Golden Thread is none of that. It's a free community for people who felt something real — and want to be in a room with others who felt it too.

"The thread does not ask which door you used. It asks if you followed it."
— The Nine Doors Home
